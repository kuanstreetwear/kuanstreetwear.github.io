Block 05 — Sài Gòn

Field notes from District 5, turned into a wearable graphic. A study of apartment blocks, signage, and everyday infrastructure.

2021Sài Gòn, VN#graphic #field-notes #district-5
区KSW-001 / COVER

Base item

A heavyweight cotton blank tee, sourced locally in Sài Gòn. Boxy cut, mid-weight, chosen for proportion rather than branding.

Idea

District 5 is dense, layered, and unmistakably itself — tiled apartment blocks, hand-painted shop signs, and tangled infrastructure. The idea was to translate that visual rhythm into a single graphic that reads like a field note, not a logo.

Modifications

  • Back graphic built from a grid of apartment-block windows
  • Stencilled coordinates and the code «КSW-001» at the hem
  • Small woven archive label added at the side seam
  • Pre-washed and sun-aged to match the local climate

Result

A quiet, wearable document of a neighbourhood. It works on the street, photographs well in daylight, and ages the way the city does — slowly, honestly.

Notes

One-off study. Not produced in quantity. The graphic and process are archived here for reference and future iterations.
