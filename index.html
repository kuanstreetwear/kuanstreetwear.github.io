Story / Introduction
SÀI GÒN

How it started

Kuan Streetwear began after visiting Vietnam in 2020. I was drawn to the raw energy, the visual noise, and the resourcefulness in everyday style.

Later, I returned to Saigon and continued this project in a smaller, more realistic format — learning, experimenting, and documenting the process.
