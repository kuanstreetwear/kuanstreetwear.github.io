About

A personal streetwear project and visual archive based in Sài Gòn — about clothes, cities, and the details in between.

Hi, I'm Kuan. Kuan Streetwear is my personal clothing modification project and visual archive. It explores local streetwear culture, post-Soviet utility aesthetics, and quiet technological details through small custom clothing projects.

It started after a trip to Vietnam in 2020 and now continues in Sài Gòn — not as a store, but as a place to make, document, and slowly build a visual identity. Maybe it grows into a real clothing brand later. For now, it's a living design project.
